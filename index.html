About the company

Who we are

Tariq Chehardy LLC is a digital venture company founded on one conviction: great software ventures come from small, senior, shipping teams with real ownership — not committees.

We originate, build, and operate digital products end to end — identifying opportunities, shipping production-grade software, and scaling what works.
