Profile

A private engineering company.

One discipline, applied everywhere: software should be engineered, not assembled.

Tariq Chehardy LLC was founded in 2020 in California and now operates from Dallas, Texas. It is a private, founder-led company with a deliberately narrow structure: no bench, no account layer, no handoffs. The person who scopes the work is the person who builds it, and every line of code in the company carries the same standard.

The work falls into three kinds of engagement. Clients arrive with systems that need to exist: custom code, commissioned programming, and production tooling built to a precise specification. They arrive with systems that need to be understood: deep reverse engineering and program-internal analysis. And they arrive with systems that need to be rebuilt properly: PowerShell and internal production carried from prototype to dependable, documented reality.

The company operates in the open where it can. Products and releases are published publicly, company records are maintained on this site, and every engagement runs on written scope, fixed estimates, and direct contact with the founder. Discretion is the default for client work; transparency is the default for everything else.

Engagements

How work happens.

Four steps, no overhead. Every engagement runs the same disciplined path, from first email to final handoff.

01

Inquiry

Email the scope: what exists, what is needed, when it ships. No forms, no calls required. Questions are answered in writing.

02

Scope

A precise specification, a fixed estimate, and a deliverable list. Nothing moves until you approve it, and nothing moves beyond it.

03

Build

Engineered to production standard from the first line. Progress stays visible throughout, with no black-box periods.

Delivery

Handed over with documentation and full ownership. It works, it is documented, and it is yours.

Commissioned work stays commissioned. Client systems stay client systems, always.
